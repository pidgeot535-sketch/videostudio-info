A private creator workspace

From a video idea to an approved upload.

VideoStudio helps its operator plan original videos, prepare scripts, assemble recorded footage and graphics, review edits, and export finished videos. It supports short videos, long videos, and paired versions.

Plan

Research topics and public video information, develop ideas, and review scripts and filming requests.

Produce

Organize your media, create an editable scene plan, review previews and captions, and export a finished video locally.

Publish and learn

Approve uploads to connected YouTube channels and review authorized video analytics to inform future content.

How the YouTube connection works

VideoStudio uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. You authorize a channel through Google's sign-in and consent screens. The app uses the granted permissions to identify that channel, upload approved videos, apply approved publishing settings, and retrieve performance reports.

Uploads require your approval of the video and its publishing details. You can revoke access at any time through Google account permissions.

Private application, public information

The production workspace runs on the operator's own computer. This website provides public application information and policies; it does not provide public access to the workspace or offer account registration.

Optional integrations include Claude for research and drafting, ElevenLabs for narration, and Telegram for notifications. These services are used only when configured and enabled. Their data handling is explained in the privacy policy.
